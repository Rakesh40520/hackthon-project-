CLOUDSCOPE ANALYTICS, INC.
MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between CloudScope Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Vendor"), and Meridian Retail Group, LLC, a New York limited liability company ("Customer").

1. SERVICES
Vendor will provide the Customer with access to the CloudScope Analytics Platform as described in Order Form A (the "Services").

2. TERM
The initial term of this Agreement is twelve (12) months, beginning on the Effective Date (the "Initial Term").

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
3.1 Setup Fee. Customer shall pay a one-time setup fee of $10,000, invoiced upon execution of this Agreement.
3.2 Subscription Fee. Customer shall pay a subscription fee of $2,000 per month for access to the Services.
3.3 Usage-Based Fees. Analytics queries in excess of 1,000,000 per month are billed at $0.002 per query.
3.4 Minimum Commitment. Customer commits to a minimum total contract value of $24,000 over the Initial Term.
3.5 Discounts. No discounts are offered under this Agreement.
3.6 Payment Terms. All invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt (Net 30).

4. RENEWAL
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

5. PRICE INCREASES
Vendor may increase the subscription fee upon renewal by no more than five percent (5%) per renewal cycle. Price increases apply only at renewal and never during the Initial Term.

6. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party. Either party may also terminate for material breach if the breach remains uncured for fifteen (15) days after written notice of the breach.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Vendor's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Customer under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

8. DATA PROCESSING AND SECURITY
Vendor will process Customer data in accordance with the Data Processing Addendum attached as Exhibit A. Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Vendor maintains SOC 2 Type II certification and will notify Customer of any confirmed data breach within seventy-two (72) hours.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

10. GENERAL
This Agreement, including its exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions.
